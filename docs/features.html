EasyServer installs the official BeamMP server and gives you a dashboard for everything else - from your PC or your phone. No config files, no command line.

⚡Set up in 5 minutes

A guided installer does the hard parts.

  • Asks for your AuthKey and checks it
  • Opens the port on your router in one click
  • Starts with your PC, restarts if it crashes

📱Dashboard, even on your phone

Start, stop, change the map and see who's online.

  • Works in any browser, also from your phone
  • Discord bot and phone notifications
  • Backups, restore and config history

🧩Mods, virus-checked

Drag & drop mods or install them from the BeamNG repository.

  • Every mod is scanned before players get it
  • Light mode: players only download the map in play
  • Share all your mods with one short code

🚗Vehicle rules NEW

Decide what can be spawned on your server.

  • Block props, giant physics props, trailers or any car
  • Heavy, laggy mod cars are found automatically
  • Stops spawn spam. Presets: Racing, Roleplay, No chaos

🔌Server plugins NEW

Add game modes made by the community.

  • CareerMP, freeRoamMP, BeamJoy and more
  • Downloaded from each author's own page, you see what goes where
  • Update, turn off or remove in one click

👥Run it as a team NEW

Friends help without knowing your password.

  • Invite links that expire after 1 hour
  • Viewer, moderator, admin - or your own roles
  • Two-step login for logins from outside your home

🛡️Players & moderation

Keep your server friendly.

  • Kick, mute and ban from the dashboard, Discord or chat
  • Invite list, anti-bot and maintenance mode
  • Player stats, busiest hours and records

🩺"Why can't my friend join?"

A health page that finds the problem for you.

  • Checks AuthKey, port, firewall and the server list
  • Explains errors in plain words
  • Weak-PC preset for old laptops

🔒Safe by default

Built so you don't have to be a security expert.

  • One-click security presets, from Most secure to Open
  • Your dashboard isn't on the internet unless you turn it on
  • Two-step login for the owner, too

Ready to host?

Download, double-click, follow the steps. Your server can be online in about 5 minutes.
