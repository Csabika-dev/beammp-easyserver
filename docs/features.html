Host BeamMP for your friends without touching a config file

EasyServer installs the official BeamMP server, then gives you one page to run it from. It's free, open source and works on Windows and Linux.

Download How to install

Getting it onlineThe part most people get stuck on.

Guided setup

Paste your AuthKey and the installer checks it's valid. It sets the server to start with your PC and restarts it if it crashes.

Opening the port

One click asks your router to open port 30814. If your router doesn't allow that, the Help page explains how to do it by hand.

"Why can't my friend join?"

The Health page checks your AuthKey, the port, the firewall and whether your server shows up in the BeamMP list, and tells you which one is wrong.

Old laptops welcome

The Weak computer preset allows fewer players and cars and clears abandoned cars quickly, so a spare laptop can run a small server.

Running itFrom your desk or from the sofa.

Start, stop, change map

Change the map from a list instead of editing ServerConfig.toml. Works in any browser, including on your phone.

Players and bans

Kick, mute and ban from the dashboard, from Discord or with a chat command. There's also an invite-only list and maintenance mode.

Mods

Drag a zip onto the page or pick one from the BeamNG repository. Each one is virus-scanned before players download it.

Share your whole mod list with a short code, and a friend's server can install the same set.

Backups and alerts

Back up and restore your config. Get a message on Discord, email, Telegram or your phone when the server crashes.

New in 1.15In the Rolling version now. They move to Stable once they've been tested.

Vehicle rules

Block props, the huge physics props, trailers or any car you pick. Mod cars heavy enough to lag everyone are spotted automatically.

There's a spawn cooldown too, and presets for racing and roleplay servers.

Server plugins

Install community game modes like CareerMP, freeRoamMP and BeamJoy. They download from each author's own page, and you can see which files go where before you install.

Run it as a team

Send a friend an invite link that works once and stops working after an hour. Give them a role, like viewer or moderator, for each server.

Anyone who logs in from outside your home network needs a two-step login code.

Try it on your PC

Download it, run it and follow the steps. Most people are online in about five minutes. If you get stuck, the Help page and the community can help.
